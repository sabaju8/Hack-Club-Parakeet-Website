Parakeets

Parakeets are small birds native to Australia. They are characterized by long tails, zygodactyl feet (meaning they have 4 toes), and various color mutations. Most wild parakeets are bright green or yellow, but other colors such as blue, white, yellow, and violet have developed through captive breeding.

There are many types of parakeets, including cockatiels, monk parakeets, indian ringnecks, and more.
